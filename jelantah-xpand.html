Jelantah Xpand

Expanding our environmental mission through regional exhibitions, international summits, and strategic youth collaborations.

May — The Cornerstone

Youth Conference & Exhibition

Participated as an exhibitor at The Cornerstone by EduALL, a national youth conference with 500+ participants. Through our booth, we introduced Jelantah Jenius’ UCO collection initiative, explained the environmental impact of improper oil disposal, and promoted the potential of UCO as a reusable resource.

We also connected with people from diverse fields, including doctors, business professionals, educators, and students, allowing us to exchange perspectives and expand our network. This helped bring Jelantah Jenius’ mission beyond the school community and promote responsible UCO management to a wider audience.

July — International Green Youth Leaders Summit

Global Environmental Leadership

Participated in the International Green Youth Leaders Summit, connecting Jelantah Jenius with a wider network of young environmental leaders and sustainability advocates from 12+ countries.

We shared our experience in promoting UCO collection and circular economy practices while learning from other youth-led initiatives. The summit allowed us to expand our reach, exchange ideas, and inspire greater youth participation in responsible waste management and environmental action.
