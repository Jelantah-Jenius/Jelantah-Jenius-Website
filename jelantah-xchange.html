Jelantah Xchange

Expanding our network and exposure by participating in conferences, summits, and collaborative external events.

Initiative Overview

Building Connections

Jelantah Xchange is Jelantah Jenius’ initiative to expand our network and exposure by participating in conferences, summits, exhibitions, competitions, workshops, and other external events.

Through these opportunities, members can represent Jelantah Jenius, connect with new communities, exchange ideas, and discover potential collaborations that can further our mission and impact.

July — Jelantah Jenius × MauTau

Interactive Educational Collaboration

Collaborated with MauTau at Panti Asuhan Mizan Amanah, Tanah Baru, introducing children to UCO, its environmental impact, and how it can be repurposed into candles.

We explained the candle-making process while engaging the children through interactive and creative activities, including a coloring session, making environmental education more fun and accessible.
