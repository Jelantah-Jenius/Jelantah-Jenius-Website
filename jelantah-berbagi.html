Jelantah Berbagi

Sharing knowledge and giving back to the community by teaching the value of sustainability and repurposing waste.

July — Educational Outreach

Interactive Learning & Candle Making

Visited Panti Asuhan Mizan Amanah Tanah Baru as part of Jelantah Berbagi to introduce children to the potential of UCO through an interactive educational activity. We demonstrated how used cooking oil can be transformed into candles and allowed participants to decorate their own candles.

By combining education with hands-on activities, we showed that waste can be repurposed into useful products while encouraging greater awareness of sustainable practices.
