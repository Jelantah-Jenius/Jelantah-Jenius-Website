Our Initiatives

Discover how Jelantah Jenius is turning waste into sustainable opportunities through our diverse range of educational, environmental, and social programs.

Jelantah Xpand

Jelantah Xpand is an expansion initiative designed to broaden the organization's reach by participating in educational forums, exhibitions, and interactive events. We engage with students, experts, and the broader community to exchange knowledge.

Learn More

Jumat Jelantah

Jumat Jelantah (JJ) is introduced as a weekly Used Cooking Oil (UCO) collection program at Sekolah Bogor Raya. By providing an accessible collection point every Friday, the program encourages students, teachers, and staff to dispose of used cooking oil responsibly.

Learn More

Jelantah Xchange

Jelantah Xchange is an outreach program centered on social impact and collaborative engagement. Through monthly partnerships, it brings people together to support underserved communities through joint activities and direct donations.

Learn More

Jelantah Berbagi

Jelantah Berbagi is a community-focused initiative designed to distribute social aid and foster meaningful connections with local communities, providing essential support and resources to those in need.

Learn More

Jelantah News

Jelantah News is an educational media initiative dedicated to raising public awareness about environmental sustainability. Through engaging digital content, it keeps the community informed on local ecological issues.

Learn More

Oil Runs

Oil Runs is a hands-on collection and logistics program focused on gathering used cooking oil directly from households and local businesses by establishing scheduled pickup routes and accessible drop-off points.
