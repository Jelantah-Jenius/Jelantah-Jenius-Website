Jumat Jelantah

Jumat Jelantah (JJ) is introduced as a weekly Used Cooking Oil (UCO) collection program at Sekolah Bogor Raya. By providing an accessible collection point every Friday, the program encourages students, teachers, and staff to dispose of used cooking oil responsibly while supporting environmental sustainability, the circular economy, and community participation.

The objectives of Jumat Jelantah (JJ) are to:

1. Raise awareness about the environmental and health impacts of improper UCO disposal.
2. Provide an accessible and sustainable UCO collection system for the school community.
3. Encourage responsible waste management and environmental stewardship.
4. Support the recycling of UCO into renewable energy

Activities:

All the cooking oil will be used for our programs here at Jelantah Jenius, such as: Candle making from used cooking oil; education and outreach regarding the recycling of used cooking oil; and donation programs with funds collected from used cooking oil

Jelantah Xpand

Jelantah Xpand is an expansion initiative designed to broaden the organization's reach by participating in educational forums, exhibitions, and interactive events. Through this program, Jelantah Jenius engages with students, experts, and the broader community to exchange knowledge and raise awareness about sustainable waste management.

Jelantah Xchange

Jelantah Xchange is an outreach program centered on social impact and collaborative engagement. Through monthly partnerships with local organizations, businesses, and youth groups, Jelantah Xchange brings people together to support underserved communities through joint activities, educational sharing, and direct donations.

Jelantah Berbagi

Jelantah Berbagi is a community-focused initiative by Jelantah Jenius designed to distribute social aid and foster meaningful connections with local communities. Through direct engagement and collaborative outreach, the program provides essential support and resources to those in need while raising awareness about environmental sustainability.

Jelantah News

Jelantah News is an educational media initiative dedicated to raising public awareness about environmental sustainability, waste management, and social impact. Through engaging digital content, news updates, and informative posts, the program keeps the community informed on local ecological issues and highlights key milestones in sustainable action.

Oil Runs

Oil Runs is a hands-on collection and logistics program focused on gathering used cooking oil directly from households, local businesses, and community partners. By establishing scheduled pickup routes and accessible drop-off points, the initiative ensures that waste oil is safely retrieved and diverted away from local waterways and drainage systems.
