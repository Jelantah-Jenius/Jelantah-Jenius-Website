Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build a society where cooking oil is used safely, responsibly, and sustainably, preventing environmental damage and transforming waste into renewable resources.

Our Mission

Raising public awareness, providing collection systems, and partnering with industries to transform oil into clean energy while supporting community waste-to-income initiatives.
