The Environmental Impact of Transforming Used Cooking Oil into a Primary Resource

What The Effect of the Environment if Used Cooking Oil Become The Main Resource

If used cooking oil were made a primary resource, the habit of dumping it down the drain could be reduced, thus preventing pipe blockages, reducing the burden on wastewater treatment plants, and reducing river pollution. On a Jakarta scale, this has the potential to improve water quality, reduce odors, and reduce the risk of flooding caused by grease-clogged drainage channels.

Source: Pemanfaatan limbah minyak jelantah

How Used Cooking Oil Can Change From Waste to a Resource

Even after use, used cooking oil still has value. Research from IPB University shows that the main ingredient in biodegradable materials, soaps, and candles is made from this unused oil. This proves that kitchen waste actually has economic potential if managed and handled properly.

Source: Jurnal IPB, Pemanfaatan Minyak Jelantah

The Role of Used Cooking Oil in Advancing Low-Carbon Fuel Systems as the Resource

According to BPPT, biodiesel produced from used cooking oil can significantly reduce carbon emissions compared to fossil diesel because it recycles waste and lowers life-cycle greenhouse gas output. This fuel contains circular and low carbon, it also supports the community in reducing dependence on imported petroleum while contributing to national energy.

Source: BPPT/BRIN, Biodiesel dari Minyak Jelantah
Source: Pertamina, Pengembangan Biofuel

Used Cooking Oil as a Resource for Community-Based Economic Empowerment

Used cooking oil can be a valuable product that supports micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) in the local economy. In some areas, communities have successfully converted used cooking oil into decorative and aromatherapy candles, which have high sales value as creative products. Community service projects, such as candle-making training from used cooking oil, demonstrate that these activities provide new skills for residents and create additional income opportunities.

Source: Pemanfaatan minyak jelantah sebagai program UMKM di Kelurahan Bangkinang
